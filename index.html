I

chapter IStudio

Many hands, one house.

kolektiva. is an architecture studio. We work as one team with clients, engineers and builders — from the first sketch to the last door handle.

We believe good buildings are simple to understand, honest in their materials and generous to their neighbours. Every project starts with the people and the place, never with a style.

III

chapter IIIProcess

How an idea becomes a building

From the first sketch to the last door handle.

  1. 01.

    Listen

    We begin on site and at your table. What do you need, what do you love, what can the place carry?

  2. 02.

    Draw

    Sketches, models and plans — shared early and often, so decisions are made together, not presented.

  3. 03.

    Build

    We stay close to the construction site, solving details with the people who make them.

  4. 04.

    Return

    A building is finished when it is lived in. We come back, look, and learn for the next one.
